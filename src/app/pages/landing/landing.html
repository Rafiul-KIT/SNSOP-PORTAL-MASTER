Goal and Development Objective

The primary objective of the SNSOP is to provide cash transfers, enhance income-generating opportunities, and strengthen the National Safety Net Delivery System in South Sudan.

SNSOP Core Activities

Cash Transfers

Providing direct cash assistance to vulnerable households to meet immediate consumption needs.

Economic Opportunities

Enhancing livelihood options, especially for youth, to promote productivity and self-reliance.

Institutional Strengthening

Building government capacity for Social Protection and strengthening the MIS system.

M&E and Knowledge

Managing, monitoring, and generating knowledge for informed policy-making.

Key Achievement Updates

  • 154,426 out of 157,500 households registered across 15 counties.
  • Cash Transfers launched for registered households.
  • LIPW activities created community assets and jobs.
  • Government-led MIS under rollout.

Project Components

Component 1: Cash Transfers & Social Measures ($98M)

Supports LIPW & DIS with human capital growth programs.

Component 2: Economic Opportunities ($15M)

Livelihood support and training for poor and vulnerable youth.

Component 3: Institutional Capacity ($6M)

Strengthens MGCSW & builds the national safety net delivery system.

Component 4: M&E and Knowledge ($10M)

Supports PCU for management, monitoring, and coordination.

South Sudan Context

Independence: July 9, 2011. The world’s youngest nation, facing conflict and humanitarian challenges.

Population: ~15.8M
Poverty: 76% below poverty line
Economy: Oil-dependent, high inflation
Social Security: Low coverage; SNSOP foundational
